Recyclables collected on time, weighed fairly and tracked end to end.

RecycleConnect lets households, shops, offices and institutions book a pickup for their recyclable material. It sends a collector at an agreed time and records what was actually collected, so both sides can see every step from request to completion.

The situation today

Recyclables pile up because nobody coordinates the pickup.

In most neighbourhoods, scrap is collected informally. Nobody knows when a collector will come, what they will accept, or how much was really taken. Everyone involved loses something.

Households and shops
  • No fixed pickup time, so material piles up at home or in the shop
  • Unsure what is accepted, especially e-waste and batteries
  • No record of what was handed over
Collectors
  • Wasted trips: customer not home, wrong address, material gone
  • No planned route for the day
  • Arguments about weight with nothing to back it up
Recycling organisations
  • No view of incoming requests or how much material to expect
  • Hard to assign collectors fairly across areas
  • No reliable reports on collection volume
The city
  • Recyclable material ends up in mixed waste and landfill
  • Hazardous items like batteries are handled carelessly
  • No data to plan where more collection is needed

What RecycleConnect does

It is a coordination and tracking platform. It does not replace collectors. It gives them, their customers and the organisation running collections one shared, reliable process.

What it deliberately does not do

  • No AI waste recognition or environmental prediction
  • No paid maps, SMS or payment gateway
  • No automatic hazardous-material decisions or carbon calculations

This keeps the first version free to run and focused on the pickup itself.

How it works

One request moves through eight clear steps.

Each step is visible to the people involved, and each change is recorded with who made it and when.

  1. List materialPick categories and rough quantitiesCustomer
  2. Request pickupAddress on the map, preferred day and timeCustomer
  3. AcceptChecked against what can be collectedAdmin
  4. ScheduleA slot inside working hoursAdmin
  5. PickupCollector accepts, travels, arrivesCollector
  6. Weigh and recordActual quantity, with an optional photoCollector
  7. ConfirmCustomer agrees or reports an issueCustomer
  8. CompleteSaved to history and reportsSystem

Customer

Phone first
  • Register as a household, shop, office, institution or business
  • Book pickups with several materials at once
  • Track status, confirm collection, raise complaints
  • See history and total kilograms recycled

Collector

Phone, used on the move
  • See today’s pickups on a route map
  • Accept and update each pickup step by step
  • Record actual quantity and a photo
  • Mark a failed pickup with a reason

Admin

Desktop or tablet
  • Review, accept or reject requests
  • Schedule and assign the nearest collector
  • Manage customers, collectors, categories and complaints
  • Analytics, reports with export, and an audit log

Built-in rules

The platform enforces the rules that make collection reliable.

Working hours only

Pickups can be scheduled only on working days and inside time slots (Mon–Sat, 08:00–18:00, set by the admin).

Validation

Both quantities are kept

The customer’s estimate and the collector’s actual weight are stored side by side, and the customer confirms the final figure.

Quantity confirmation

Cancellation cutoff

Customers can cancel until a set time before pickup. Every cancellation records the reason, the person and the time.

Cancellation

Failed pickups stay on record

A failed pickup needs a reason and remains in history, so repeated problems are easy to spot.

Failed pickup

Privacy by role

Customers see only their own requests. Collectors see only their assigned pickups. Phone numbers are partly hidden.

Security and privacy

Everything is logged

Accepting, scheduling, assigning, rescheduling and cancelling are all written to an audit log. Completed requests can’t be edited.

Audit log
Suggested additions

Small, free additions that fit how collection works on the ground.

These go beyond the written brief. Each costs nothing to run, and we can include or drop them after your feedback.

Tamil and English

A language switch, so customers and collectors can use the app in the language they read most comfortably.

Free notifications

In-app notifications plus optional email, instead of paid SMS. Customers get a reminder the evening before their pickup.

Works with weak signal

The collector app saves step updates and weights on the phone and sends them when the signal returns.

Zone-based assignment

Each collector has a home zone. When scheduling, the nearest collector with free capacity is suggested first.
